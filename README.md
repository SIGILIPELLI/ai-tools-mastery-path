# AI Tools Mastery Path

A free, structured, module-wise training program on evaluating and using AI
tools effectively — entry level to master level, with real frameworks,
rubrics, and worked scenarios in every module. Focused on the **broad
landscape** of AI tools and how to choose between them, not a deep dive on
any single vendor (see
[Claude Training Mastery Path](https://sigilipelli.github.io/claude-training-mastery-path/)
for that) and not hands-on ML engineering (see
[AI/ML Mastery Path](https://sigilipelli.github.io/ai-ml-mastery-path/) for
the technical track).

**Live site:** https://sigilipelli.github.io/ai-tools-mastery-path/

## Contents

- **Level 1 · Entry** — the landscape of AI tool categories, choosing the
  right tool for a task, chat assistants, writing/research, coding
  assistance, image/media generation, productivity & automation, prompting
  fundamentals, evaluating AI output critically, and a personal AI toolkit
  project
- **Level 2 · Intermediate** — systematic tool comparison, combining tools
  into workflows, research and content-creation pipelines, automation
  platforms, privacy & security, cost-benefit analysis, personal routines,
  common pitfalls
- **Level 3 · Advanced** — team adoption, internal guidelines, vendor
  evaluation, integrating tools into existing stacks, ROI measurement,
  change management, data governance, training teams, staying current
- **Level 4 · Master** — organizational AI strategy, governance committees,
  vendor risk management, scaling adoption, ethics at scale, AI maturity
  measurement, centers of excellence, career growth to AI tools
  strategist/consultant
