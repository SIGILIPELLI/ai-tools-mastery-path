# AI Tools Mastery Path

A structured, module-wise training program on the **broad landscape of AI
tools** and how to choose and use them well — from your first look at
chat assistants and image generators to running AI tool strategy for an
entire organization. Every module ends with a real framework, rubric, or
worked scenario you can apply directly, plus a hands-on exercise.

This track is about **AI tool literacy**: knowing what categories of tools
exist, how to evaluate and choose between them, how to combine them into
real workflows, and how to govern their use responsibly. It stays deliberately
general about specific products and pricing (both change constantly) and
focuses instead on durable judgment. If you want a deep dive on one
specific assistant, see [Claude Training Mastery Path](https://sigilipelli.github.io/claude-training-skillmastery/).
If you want the hands-on technical track for building and training models
yourself, see [AI/ML Mastery Path](https://sigilipelli.github.io/ai-ml-skillmastery/).

## How the program is organized

| Level | Focus | Modules |
|-------|-------|---------|
| [Level 1 · Entry](level-1/index.md) | The landscape of AI tool categories, choosing tools for a task, chat assistants, writing/research, coding, image/media, productivity, prompting, evaluating output | 9 topics + 1 project |
| [Level 2 · Intermediate](level-2/index.md) | Systematic comparison, combining tools into workflows, research and content pipelines, automation platforms, privacy, cost-benefit analysis | 9 topics + 1 project |
| [Level 3 · Advanced](level-3/index.md) | Team adoption, internal guidelines, vendor evaluation, integrating tools into stacks, ROI, change management, governance | 9 topics + 1 project |
| [Level 4 · Master](level-4/index.md) | Organizational AI strategy, governance committees, vendor risk, scaling adoption, ethics at scale, centers of excellence, career growth | 9 topics + 1 capstone |

## How to use this site

- Work through each level in order — later modules assume earlier ones.
- Every topic page includes a concrete framework or rubric (formatted as a
  table), plus a worked scenario showing it applied to a real decision.
- Each topic ends with an **Exercise** — a practical deliverable using your
  own workflow, team, or organization as the subject.
- Each level ends with a project that combines everything learned in that
  level into a real artifact: a personal toolkit, a workflow design, an
  adoption proposal, or a full organizational strategy.
- Use the search bar (top of the page) to jump straight to a topic.

Start here → [Level 1 · Entry](level-1/index.md)

## Who this is for

Anyone who wants to use AI tools well without chasing every new release —
individual contributors building a personal AI toolkit, team leads deciding
what to adopt, and anyone who eventually needs to set AI tool policy for a
team or organization. No coding background required.
